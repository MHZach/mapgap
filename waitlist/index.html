01 · What's in the beta

Four instruments, one evidence base

Each module answers a question that conventional analytics cannot: not whether someone arrived on your site, but what an AI system told them beforehand — and whether that representation is moving for or against you.

Module 01

Sentiment Analysis

Multi-axis rather than a single polarity score: evaluative tone, confidence, commercial framing and brand voice can all point in different directions on the very same mention.
